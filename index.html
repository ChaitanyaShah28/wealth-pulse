Mutual Funds vs. Direct Stocks — Which Should You Choose?

Recommended split: 80–85% Equity Mutual Funds as your core engine + 15–20% Direct Stocks only if you enjoy company research

Factor Equity Mutual Funds (SIPs) Direct Stocks
Expected Return 12% – 14% p.a. (Consistent) 14% – 18%+ p.a. (or <8% if mistimed)
Risk & Drawdowns Moderate-High — 40–60 stocks cushion single-company crashes High — single stock can drop 30%–60%
Time & Effort Zero daily effort — fully automated monthly SIPs 5–10+ hrs/week tracking earnings & valuations
Tax Efficiency (India) High — no tax on internal rebalancing until you redeem Lower if churning — every sale triggers STCG/LTCG tax
